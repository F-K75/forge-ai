# The Forge Team

## Our Mission

Forge exists to make building software as easy as describing an idea. We believe anyone should be able to create production-ready applications, businesses, and intelligent systems with AI, without being limited by engineering complexity or team size.

## Founders

### Keshav Frendia — Founder & CEO

Keshav Frendia is the founder of Forge, an AI-native software development platform designed to transform ideas into production-ready products through intelligent multi-agent collaboration. With a background in data engineering, AI systems, and automation, he has spent years building scalable platforms, developer tools, and AI workflows that remove the friction between imagination and execution.

He founded Forge with a simple belief: the future of software development isn't writing more code, it's orchestrating intelligent systems that can design, build, test, deploy, and improve products alongside humans.
