Launch to a specific audience with one clear message, staged exposure, ready support, and launch metrics agreed in advance.

## Checklist
- [ ] Positioning: target customer, problem, alternative they use today, and why this is better.
- [ ] One-sentence value proposition and three supporting proof points.
- [ ] Choose the launch tier (silent, minor, major) based on impact and risk.
- [ ] Stage exposure: internal dogfood, beta/early access, general availability.
- [ ] Prepare assets: release notes, changelog entry, docs, demo, in-app announcement.
- [ ] Brief support and sales with FAQs, known limitations, and escalation paths.
- [ ] Confirm operational readiness: flags, monitoring, rollback, capacity for the spike.
- [ ] Define launch metrics (activation, adoption, conversion) and review them at day 1, 7, and 30.
- [ ] Collect qualitative feedback from beta users and feed it into the roadmap.

## Pitfalls
- Announcing features before they are reliably available to everyone targeted.
- Messaging that lists features instead of customer outcomes.
- No owner for post-launch follow-up.
